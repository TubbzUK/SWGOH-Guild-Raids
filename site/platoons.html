Simulate the territory battle

Plays the TB through phase by phase with your guild's roster and the rules in the menu. Each phase it tries every chosen operation that's open and not done yet, keeps the set the guild can fill completely, and holds the rest for a later phase when everyone's units are free again (a unit can be placed once per phase). It then shows which operations to fill in which phase, and the gaps: operations nobody can fill yet, and the upgrades that would unlock them.

Rules

Preset to R5 through R9. Check against the current in-game requirements; a Relic column in the plan overrides these.

Ranks members by total GP into four equal tiers, tier 1 being the highest. GL slots go to tier 1 first, then down the tiers. Tier 1 gets slightly more platoons than tier 4 (about 20% above an even share for tier 1, 10% for tier 2, an even share for tier 3, 10% below for tier 4), and the per-member limit scales the same way.

Every slot is filled first within these limits. Any slot still empty is then refilled from whoever qualifies, going over the limits only where needed and spreading the extra as evenly as possible.

When a member is given the lead unit of a squad (say Executor), the other units of that squad needed in the same phase go to them as well, as far as they own them. They can't use the squad in that TB anyway, so nobody else loses theirs. This can take them past the per-member limit above. Members already placing part of a squad are also preferred for the rest of it.

Preset with common fleets. Edit them to match how your guild runs its squads; character squads work too (for example Lord Vader: Darth Vader, Admiral Piett).
