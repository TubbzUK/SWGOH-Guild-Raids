Rules

Preset to R5 through R9. Check against the current in-game requirements; a Relic column in the plan overrides these.

Ranks members by total GP into four equal tiers, tier 1 being the highest. GL slots go to tier 1 first, then down the tiers. Tier 1 gets slightly more platoons than tier 4 (about 20% above an even share for tier 1, 10% for tier 2, an even share for tier 3, 10% below for tier 4), and the per-member limit scales the same way.

Every slot is filled first within these limits. Any slot still empty is then refilled from whoever qualifies, going over the limits only where needed and spreading the extra as evenly as possible.
